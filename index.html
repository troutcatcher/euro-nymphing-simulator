How to fish it

Cast with click or Space. The flies tuck in upstream and start sinking.

Lead the sighter downstream at the speed of the water. Contact wants to sit in the marked band: tight enough that a take moves the sighter, loose enough that you are not towing the flies.

Get down. Fish eat within about 30 cm of the bed. Deeper or faster water needs a heavier bead, a longer leader, or a cast further upstream.

Strike at anything the sighter does that the current did not do — a stall, a tick, a lift. You have a few tenths of a second. Sweep the rod tip up (or up and downstream) to set the hook, or use click / Space. A slow raise through the drift will not trip it; a sharp one will.

Play it by holding the mouse button to gather line and releasing to give it back. Watch the tippet load.

On a phone or tablet: drag anywhere on the water to hold the rod tip, and use the round button to cast, strike, and gather line.
